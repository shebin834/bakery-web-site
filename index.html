A Legacy of Taste

Welcome to Poovil Bakery! Our story is rooted in the rich culinary heritage of Kerala, preserving authentic tastes passed down through generations. What began as a humble passion for traditional flavors has grown into a legacy of pure, uncompromised quality.

While we stay true to our nostalgic roots with our classic hot Snacks and savory Mixtures, we also embrace the modern palate with bespoke Birthday Cakes, artisanal Ice Creams, fresh Juices, and refreshing Cold Beverages. Every item we serve is a piece of our history, crafted with love, tradition, and the finest ingredients.
